HOW TO PLAY

Choose your stake and press PLAY. Sixteen balls are played, one after the other. Every ball ends in one of the 16 slots. A slot that gets a ball lights its number on your card.

Light the cells of a winning pattern on your card and it pays: ACROSS (any row), DOWN (any column), DIAGONAL, the 4 CORNERS, the SMALL BOX, LUCKY SEVEN, an L SHAPE, NUMBER ELEVEN, EQUAL, a Z SHAPE, the BIG BOX and the FULL CARD. The small drawings in the pay panel show each shape. Only your best pattern pays; patterns do not add up. The most common patterns pay half your stake.

Jackpot: five numbers, shown as JP NUMS, are drawn for every play. If each of the five has caught at least two balls you win the jackpot pool, on top of any pattern pay. A share of every stake is added to the pool, which is shared by all players of the game.

The plunger is for show. The result of every play is decided by the server before the first ball is launched. How hard or how long you pull the plunger never changes where the balls go or what you win.

There are at least 3 seconds between two plays. The balance shown is the one of your account. Win paid is added to your balance as soon as the platform confirms it.

Every play is provably fair: press FAIR to see the commitment to the next draws and to check past plays yourself.

18+. Gambling can be addictive. Play only what you can afford to lose.

FAIR PLAY

Before your first play the server committed to a secret number (the server seed) by showing you its fingerprint. Every result comes from that secret, your own client seed and a play counter, so the server cannot change a result after seeing your stake. When you start a new seed, the old secret is shown and you can check every play made with it, here in your browser.
