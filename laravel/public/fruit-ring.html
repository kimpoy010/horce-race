HOW TO PLAY

Put credits on the fruits you like (tap a fruit to add one credit, hold it to take its credits off). Choose what one credit is worth, then press START. A light runs round the ring and stops on one of its 24 cells. Every cell that lights pays the credits you put on its fruit, as the table below shows (your credits times the number, your stake included). A cell marked ×3 pays three times.

Lucky cells pay nothing themselves but start free spins on the same bets: the purple one gives 3 free spins, the cyan one one free spin with 3 lit cells. Free spins never give more free spins.

Double up: after a win you can try to double it. Pick SMALL (1-7) or BIG (8-14): a number from 1 to 14 is drawn, the odds are exactly even. Right: the win doubles. Wrong: it is lost. With the left and right arrows you can first move part of the win from BONUS-WIN to your CREDIT: that part is paid now and is not at risk, only what stays in BONUS-WIN is doubled. You can double up to 3 times in a row, then the win is collected. Press COLLECT at any time to take it; a win you leave alone is collected for you after a couple of minutes, or when you start the next spin.

The result is decided by the server before the light starts running. The animation is only for show.

The balance shown is the one of your account. A win is added to your balance when you collect it.

Every spin and every double-up is provably fair: press FAIR to see the commitment to the next draws and to check past plays yourself.

18+. Gambling can be addictive. Play only what you can afford to lose.

FAIR PLAY

Before your first spin the server committed to a secret number (the server seed) by showing you its fingerprint. Every spin and every double-up comes from that seed, your own client seed and a counter, so the server cannot change a result after seeing your bet. When you start a new seed the old server seed is shown and you can check every play made with it. The check below runs in your browser.
